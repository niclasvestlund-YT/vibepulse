# Deferred features

The current scope is the round VibePulse quota design and an eventual static
panel/touch bring-up. Audio, RTC, IMU rotation, battery telemetry, TF storage,
BLE and optional GPS are vendor-listed capabilities, not validated product
features. None is implied by successful native rendering.

The important round-specific opportunity is five touch targets inside the
visible disk: north, east, south, west and centre. Rectangular corner targets
would sit outside the glass. Each target has its own visible counter.

Keep the labels of reset and forecast-to-empty countdowns coupled to the
selected deadline. A new countdown shape must not silently change its meaning.
