# Ambient Signal

## Philosophy

There is a class of information that should not be read, only *noticed*. It
lives in the dark at the edge of vision, costs nothing to ignore, and earns
attention only at the moment it matters. Ambient Signal is the aesthetic of
that threshold — the discipline of designing for the glance rather than the
stare, for the room rather than the screen.

The movement begins with true black. Not near-black, not charcoal: the
absolute absence of emitted light, where a surface stops being a rectangle
and becomes a window. Everything placed on that black appears to float,
unmoored from any frame, because there is no frame — only the lit and the
unlit. This is not a stylistic preference but a material fact borrowed from
emissive display, and it must be honoured with painstaking precision: a
single stray grey, one lazy container fill, and the illusion collapses into
an ordinary dark theme. The craft is in what is *not* drawn.

Colour arrives as evidence, never decoration. In a field of black, a warm
ember and a cool indigo are not palette choices — they are meanings, each
belonging to one thing and one thing only, applied with the restraint of a
cartographer assigning ink to territory. Light bleeds outward from lit
matter as it does in the physical world: a diffuse halo, barely perceptible,
that tells the eye an object is a source rather than a sticker. These glows
must be calibrated over many iterations until they are felt but never seen —
the difference between a masterwork and a mockup is measured in single
percentage points of alpha.

Scale carries the hierarchy that colour refuses to. One element must be
unmistakably first — enormous, confident, legible at the size of a thumbnail
held at arm's length — and everything else must yield to it without protest.
Between the dominant and the quiet there is no middle: the composition
speaks in a shout and a whisper, never a murmur. Numerals earn the largest
type in the system, because a number is the fastest sentence ever written.
Supporting text is set small, tracked wide, and positioned with the patience
of someone who has moved it four pixels at a time for an hour.

Depth is suggested, never faked. Objects recede through diminished scale,
softened focus and lowered luminance — the honest optics of a lens — and
never through drop shadows, bevels or perspective trickery. Two instances of
the same form at different depths tell a story that one cannot: this is a
thing that exists in numbers, in a room, in time. The rear object is
deliberately less resolved, and that restraint is precisely what makes the
front one feel real.

Above all, Ambient Signal demands the invisible labour of alignment. Every
optical edge reconciled, every gap tuned to a common rhythm, every baseline
sitting where the eye expects rather than where the arithmetic lands. The
finished work should look inevitable — as though no other arrangement was
ever possible — which is the only honest evidence of countless hours spent
by someone at the top of their field. Nothing overlaps. Nothing crowds the
boundary. Nothing is approximate.

## Expression rules

- True black `#000000` ground; no container ever lighter than `#0A0A0B`.
- Two meaning-bearing accents only. Everything else is white or grey.
- Glows: radial, wide, under 12% peak alpha. Felt, not seen.
- One dominant element. One supporting. Nothing in between.
- Depth via scale, blur and luminance — never shadow or perspective.
- Type tracked wide when small; tight when large. Never crowded.
- Minimum 72px margin on every edge. Non-negotiable.
